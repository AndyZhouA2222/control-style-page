DESC_overview:
This website is Andy Zhou's personal online portfolio of concept art. The portfolio includes my recent world-building project, [[Black Stone]].

DETAILS_overview:
This worldview is based on the SCP Foundation and Control.
There will be some words on the page encoded with ACSII code, which can be unlocked by entering the correct word.
I modeled all the images myself with blender. AI was not involved in the production, just Photoshop for post-processing.
